ChipIdea core at usb@78d9000 on the UZ801 stick, observed 2026-10-20

Linux register state

The stick's Debian (kernel 6.7.0-rc4-msm8916) had ci_hdrc.0 running as a high speed device. The core's first 0x200 bytes were read through /dev/mem with a Python mmap, read only. Offset 0x000 was lost in capture. The registers of interest are SBUSCFG 0x090 = 0x00000000, AHBMODE 0x098 = 0x00000008, GENCONFIG 0x09c = 0x035b0820 (bit 4 clear), GENCONFIG2 0x0a0 = 0x0030ffe0 (bit 7 set), PORTSC 0x184 = 0x88000205 (PTS ULPI, high speed), USBMODE 0x1a8 = 0x0000001a (device, SLOM, SDIS).

PureDarwin state after its own controller reset

PDQcomCIUDC read these right after USBCMD.RST cleared, through a temporary IORegistry property: PORTSC 0x8c000004 (PTS already ULPI), AHBMODE 0x00000008, GENCONFIG 0x035b0830 (bit 4 set), GENCONFIG2 0x0038ff60, SBUSCFG 0x00000000, USBMODE 0x00000000.

Which writes matter

PDQcomCIUDC used to write PORTSC.PTS = ULPI, AHBMODE = 0x8, and clear GENCONFIG bit 4 after the reset. The first two match the reset values, so they changed nothing. A temporary boot-arg gated all three, with a raw bulk read of 256 MB from the pdfb pipe through pyusb on the host as the test. All three on gave 23.3 to 24.5 and 20.5 to 23.7 MB/s on two boots, all off gave 20.9 to 23.7 MB/s, and only the GENCONFIG write left off gave 20.8 to 23.9 MB/s. Enumeration at high speed, adb and the pdfb stream worked in every case. All three writes were dropped.

USBMODE.SDIS stays set. Leaving it clear (pdusbsdis=0) measured about 10 MB/s earlier on this core.
